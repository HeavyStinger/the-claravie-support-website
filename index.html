Common questions

Do I need to know what I need?

No. You do not have to know the name of a service to ask for help. Start with the problem, or take the free check-up.

Is the check-up really free?

Yes. It is free, takes about 5 minutes and shows your results right away. We never ask for tax IDs or bank details.

Do I need special software?

No. We work with the tools and systems you already use, whether that’s paper records, spreadsheets or business software. If a better system would make your work easier, we’ll help you explore practical options suited to your business, budget and comfort with technology.

Will you do my accounting or legal filings?

We do not provide independent legal advice, audits or services reserved for licensed professionals. Where specialized expertise is required, we’ll recommend working with an appropriately qualified professional.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. The Claravie Support operates online, serving businesses throughout Belize. We provide support through virtual meetings, phone calls, WhatsApp and shared digital tools.
